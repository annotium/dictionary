Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Summary: Japanese-Thai Dictionary does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All data is processed entirely on your device.

1. Information we do not collect

The app collects none of the following:

  • Name, email, or any personally identifying information
  • Search history or favourite words (stored locally on your device only)
  • Location data
  • Camera or photo data
  • Device information or IP address
  • Analytics or behavioural tracking data

2. Data stored locally

The app stores the following data directly on your device and never transmits it to any external server:

  • Search history: words you've recently looked up
  • Favourites: entries you've bookmarked
  • App settings: installed dictionary database version, display language, and Pro purchase status

This data can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app.

3. Network connections

The app works entirely offline. An internet connection is only used in the following cases:

  • Pro version: verifying purchases made through the App Store or Google Play
  • Camera OCR (Pro): a one-time download of an ML Kit model (~10 MB) from Google's servers for Japanese text recognition. Once downloaded, this feature works entirely offline. Camera images are never sent off the device.
  • Handwriting recognition (Pro): a one-time download of an ML Kit digital-ink model (~25 MB) for recognizing hand-drawn Japanese characters. Once downloaded, this feature works entirely offline. Your drawn strokes are never sent off the device.
  • Translator (Pro): a one-time download of Japanese and Thai ML Kit translation models (~60 MB combined). Once downloaded, translations happen entirely on-device.

Outside of these cases, the app makes no connection to any server. The optional stroke-order animation data is bundled inside the app itself and is not downloaded.

4. Third-party services

The app integrates the following third-party services:

  • Apple App Store / Google Play Billing: processes payments for the Pro version. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to these transactions.
  • Google ML Kit: downloads models for text recognition, handwriting recognition, and translation used by Pro features. See Google's ML Kit terms.

5. Children

The app does not collect information about anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted on this page along with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: annotium@gmail.com
