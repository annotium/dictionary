Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?
Yes. The entire dictionary is bundled on your device from install — no internet connection is needed to look up a word. Only Pro features (camera OCR, handwriting recognition, translator, voice input) need a connection the very first time, to download a small model — after that they also work offline.

Is the Pro version a subscription?
No. It's a one-time purchase that permanently unlocks all Pro features, with no recurring payment.

I got a new phone — how do I restore my Pro purchase?
Open Settings → "Restore purchase" (or the same button on the Pro screen). The app checks your App Store / Google Play account and re-enables Pro automatically.

Where do the definitions and example sentences come from?
The dictionary draws on JMdict, KANJIDIC2, KRADFILE/RADKFILE, JmdictFurigana, jlpt-word-list, the Tanaka Corpus, and KanjiVG (for stroke-order animation). Full licence and source details are available in the app, under Settings → "Sources & licenses".

Why doesn't a kanji show stroke-order animation when I tap it?
Stroke-order data (from the KanjiVG project) currently covers about 62% of kanji in the dictionary. Kanji without coverage show a "stroke order unavailable" message instead — this isn't a bug, some characters simply aren't in the source data yet.

I found an error in a definition or example sentence.
Please let us know by email with the word in question — we can't fix upstream sources without a user report.
